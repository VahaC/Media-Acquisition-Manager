## Highlights

**This update will show you which of your movie and episode files are duplicates, and why. It is
still being built: nothing in the app has changed yet.** It will look through your media folders,
count a file once however many names it has, compare files byte for byte where that is worth it,
and tell exact copies apart from other versions of the same movie or episode and from files that
only might be the same. Nothing will be deleted, moved or renamed. See
[Coming in this update](#coming-in-this-update).

## Coming in this update

- Your series, seasons and episodes from Jellyfin beside your movies on the **Library** page, from
  every library, mixed ones included.
- A look through your media folders for every movie and episode file, the ones Jellyfin doesn't list
  included.
- A **Duplicates** page that groups exact copies, other versions of the same movie or episode, and
  possible matches, with the evidence for each group.

## Your files

Everything this update adds only reads. The app goes by your library as it is: a file Jellyfin lists
as a movie stays a movie, even when its name says it is an episode.
